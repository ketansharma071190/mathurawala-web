Govardhan Puja · Tue 10 Nov

Braj ka Annakut, ghar tak.

After Diwali, Braj does not light more lamps. It cooks.

On Govardhan Puja, every home in Mathura sends its best dish to the foot of Govardhan, and the offerings rise into a hill of food. That hill is the Annakut, and the day is Braj's own festival.

Every child in Braj learns why, and learns it as itihasa, our history. Shri Krishna lifted Govardhan and held it over the village for seven days, to shelter Braj from the rain, and for those seven days he went without his eight meals a day. When the rain stopped, Braj made up every one of them.

Seven days. Eight meals. Fifty-six dishes.

Mathura peda has been on that hill for as long as anyone remembers. Mathurawala is a Mathura kitchen, so this is the Diwali we bring to Pune: every box a piece of that hill, made in our kitchen in Baner by a halwai from Mathura. Bharat ka asli swaad.

Yes. Delivery is free in Baner, Balewadi, Aundh, Pashan, Sus, Bavdhan, Wakad and Hinjewadi Phase 1 on orders from Rs 1,001, Rs 79 below that, and Rs 149 anywhere else in Pune. What is in the Annakut Hamper?

A 500 g Braj Meetha Box (kaju katli, Mathura peda, desi ghee besan laddoo and desi ghee motichoor laddoo), a 250 g Mathura Peda box and 250 g of jeera mathri or namak pare, in a cloth bag with the Govardhan story card. It costs Rs 1,001. The Annakut Hamper Bada, Rs 1,501, adds a 250 g kaju katli box and 250 g shakkar pare.

Why is it called the Annakut?

Govardhan Puja, which follows Diwali, is Braj's own festival. On that day Mathura offers a hill of food at the foot of Govardhan, the Annakut, remembering the seven days Shri Krishna held Govardhan over the village. Mathurawala is a Mathura kitchen, so this is the Diwali story we bring to Pune.

How fresh are the sweets, and how long do they keep?

Every box is packed on the day it leaves our kitchen, and the Mathura peda is made that same morning, never a day ahead. Peda keeps 3 days at room temperature and 7 days in the fridge. Kaju katli and the desi ghee laddoo keep 10 days. Jeera mathri, namak pare and shakkar pare keep about a month. Desi kalakand keeps 2 to 3 days, so it goes out same day only.
