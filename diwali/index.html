Braj ka Annakut, ghar tak

After Diwali, Braj turns to Govardhan. When Indra’s rains beat down for seven days, Shri Krishna lifted the hill, and every family and every cow of Braj sheltered beneath it. Ever since, each home cooks what it can, and the offerings rise into a hill of food, the Annakut, shared as prasad. Our Annakut Hamper carries that feast from a Mathura kitchen to your home, with the Govardhan story card for the children.
