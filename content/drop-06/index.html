Reel script · week 11

₹35 mein Mathura — film in 5 minutes

The single highest-ROI Reel for walk-ins: value + authenticity + hunger. Total length: 10-12 seconds. One person at the counter can film this alone, phone propped on the counter.

₹35 mein Mathura · Full Script

HOOK (0-2s) — make or break

  • Text on screen: "₹35 mein Mathura." Bold. White on navy. No voiceover.
  • OR open with price tag close-up, then zoom out to the full plate.

SHOTS (2-10s)

  • 2-4s: Kachori dropping into the oil, the sizzle — 2-3 seconds.
  • 4-6s: Kachori coming out golden, placed on the plate. Sabji poured over.
  • 6-9s: Someone's hand breaking the kachori open — the steam and filling visible.
  • 9-10s: First bite. Genuine. Keep it real, no over-acting.

CLOSE (10-12s)

  • Text card: "Mathurawala, Baner. Khao toh asli khao."

CAPTION

₹35 mein Mathura ka asli kachori sabji. Made live every morning. Baner, Pune. Khao toh asli khao. #kachori #punefood #baner #mathurawala #mathura #asliswaad

AUDIO

Search "trending" on IG Reels audio, pick any track with 5k-50k uses that has energy. Don't overthink it. The visual + hook does the work.

NOTES

Film in natural daylight (near the window or outside the counter). Don't use the kitchen tube-light — it kills the food. No slow-mo needed. Normal speed, real energy. The ₹35 hook will stop the scroll on its own.
