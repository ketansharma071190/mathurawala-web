The premise

Most brands rent the festival. We are from it.

On Janmashtami, every food brand in the country posts a blue Krishna and a flute and wishes everyone a happy festival. They are borrowing the day. We are not. Mathura is Krishna's birthplace, our name is Mathurawala, and the founder's family is from there. The whole country looks at Mathura once a year, and we are the brand named after it.

That single fact runs through this entire plan. We do not chase trends with a discount sticker on top. We tell people what Mathura actually eats on these days, we show it being made by hand at the counter, and we give people far from Mathura the real thing, made the way it has always been made. Three things we have that no Pune competitor can copy:

Our edge

Real heritage, not invented

Krishna, Mathura, Braj, the family story. An emotional spine 5,000 years deep that brands like Liquid Death had to manufacture from scratch.

Our edge

Made-live theatre

The kachori frying, the jalebi coiling, the ghewar soaking, the makhan being churned. Process is the product, and the highest-reach format there is.

Our edge

A rooted, displaced audience

The rooted Bhartiya, far from the source, who knows the real thing the moment they taste it. Validated in our own reviews. Festivals are when they ache the most, and when we matter the most.

Tentpole 1

Raksha Bandhan

Friday · 28 August

Hero: Malai Ghewar

The angle. Ghewar is the Rakhi sweet of the North, a monsoon-only honeycomb soaked in rabdi and kesar. Most Pune shops won't have a real one. We do, and we make it the gift a sister sends a brother and a brother sends back. The emotional hook is distance: the bhai-behan who can't be in the same city this Rakhi. "Raakhi nahi pahuncha sakte, par mithaas toh bhej sakte ho."

Concept 1 · made-live Reel · 12s
Hook: "Iss mithai ke liye saal bhar wait karna padta hai."
Top-down, the ghewar lowered into ghee, the honeycomb forming, then the slow pour of rabdi and a rain of kesar and pista. End on the cross-section. Caption: "Sirf Sawan mein milta hai. Mathura ka malai ghewar, haath se bana, rabdi mein doob ke. Iss Rakhi, Mathura ki mithaas. 🦚 #ghewar #rakhi"
Concept 2 · heritage Carousel · 5 slides
Hook: "Ghewar sirf monsoon mein kyun banta hai?"
Slide story: what ghewar is, why Sawan, the Teej-Rakhi gifting tradition (the "sindhara" sent to daughters), how we make it, where to order. Saves bait. Caption: "Har mithai ka apna mausam hota hai. Ghewar ka mausam ab hai. Swipe karke jaaniye iski kahani. Radhe Radhe."
Concept 3 · gifting Carousel + Story
Hook: "Bhai door hai? Mithaas paas bhej do."
The Rakhi Ghewar Box, shot with the mor-pankh and a rakhi thread. Caption: "Iss Rakhi, doori ko mithaas se bharo. Mathura ka ghewar, premium box mein, aapke bhai-behan ke ghar tak. Order pickup ya delivery, WhatsApp pe. 🎁"
Concept 4 · roots & belonging Reel · 18s
Hook: "Pune mein ghewar dhoondh rahe the?"
A customer's hand unboxing the ghewar, the face, "Arre, asli wali milti hai yahan!" Real reaction, real recognition. Caption: "UP-Rajasthan se door rehne walon ke liye, asli ghewar ab Pune mein milta hai. Tag karo us insaan ko jiske saath ghewar khaana hai."
Concept 5 · counter UGC Story · poll
Hook: "Ghewar: kesar wala ya plain malai?"
A poll sticker, two photos. Soft, daily, builds the habit. Repost anyone who replies with their order. Caption (sticker): "Aaj ka fresh batch ready hai. Aap konsa lenge?"

Gifting tie-in: the Rakhi Ghewar Box

A premium ghewar box with the mor-pankh and a Mathura note, in two sizes, plus an option to pair with peda. Routed to WhatsApp and DotPe for pickup, delivered for large orders. Push the "send to your bhai-behan's city" line hard from T-10. Open pre-orders so the kitchen can count the monsoon-only batch. Every order is a direct, high-margin sale we own, not a platform commission.

Tentpole 2 · the peak

Janmashtami

Friday · 4 September

Heroes: Chappan Bhog · Mathura Peda · makhan-mishri · kachori · panjiri

The angle. This is the day. The whole pitch in one line: "The one place in Pune that eats what Mathura eats on Krishna's birthday." We do not wish people a happy Janmashtami. We show and serve the real, sattvik Braj bhog (no onion, no garlic), explain it dish by dish, and ship the travel-safe part of it (peda and dhaniya panjiri) to the homesick across India. Devotional, not gimmicky. Never a stock Krishna; always our hands, our ghee, our family. The full campaign and runway live in the Janmashtami brief; this is the content layer.

Concept 1 · the bhog series Carousel / Reels · 8 parts
Hook: "Janmashtami pe Mathura kya khata hai? Most log nahi jaante."
A series, one prasad item per post, counting down. Makhan-mishri (Kanha's favourite), dhaniya panjiri, charnamrit, the Chappan Bhog origin (Krishna's 8 meals over 7 days of Govardhan = 56). Educational, devotional, the AEO and saves engine. Caption: "Bhog Series, 1 of 8. Makhan-mishri, Kanha ka sabse pyaara bhog. Iss Janmashtami hum wahi banayenge, Mathura ke tareeke se. Radhe Radhe."
Concept 2 · made-live proof Reel · 15s
Hook: "No machine. No shortcut. Jaise Mathura mein banta hai."
The dhaniya panjiri roasting in ghee, makhan being churned by hand, peda being shaped. The made-live moat on film. Caption: "Prasad shortcut se nahi banta. Haath se, ghee mein, shraddha se. Mathura ka bhog, Pune mein. 🦚"
Concept 3 · the reveal Carousel + Reel · T-30
Hook: "4 September ko, Mathura ye khata hai. Ab Pune bhi."
Unveil the Mathura Bhog Thali and the Prasad Hamper, each item named with its meaning. Founder to camera: "Mera parivaar Mathura se hai. Ye wahi bhog hai jisme hum bade hue." Opens pre-orders. Offer tie-in: the Bhog and the Prasad Hamper

Two products, one identity. In-store and direct-delivery: the Mathura Bhog Thali (sattvik, the real Braj bhog, plated with a story card), with a low-ticket Chhota Bhog for volume. Free prasad to every walk-in. Capture the surge into the WhatsApp channel and Google reviews. Route hampers and bulk direct, not via the platforms.

Tentpole 3 · Pune's own

How often, realistically, with one outlet

A weekly rhythm a lean team can actually keep.

4-5 Reels / week

Made-live and emotion, on trending audio. The reach engine. Batch-shot in one counter session.

2 carousels / week

One heritage or Braj-knowledge, one menu or gifting. The saves and authority engine.

Daily Stories (1-3)

Today's fresh batch, polls, "guess the dish," repost every tag and UGC, behind the counter.

2-3 Google Business posts / week

And a relentless review nudge: QR at the counter and on the bill. The trust asset that compounds.

YouTube Shorts + 1 long film / month

Mirror the Reels; one founder-story or "a day at Mathurawala" film for depth and search.

Rakhi from T-10, Janmashtami from T-30 (reveal) then daily T-14, Ganesh from T-7. Post at peak windows: lunch 12-2, evening 6-9.

For Ketan · 60-90 min at the counter, on a phone

One fixed shot list, repeat it every week.

Shoot horizontal and vertical of each. Good light, steady hands, get close. This single session feeds a whole week of Reels and Stories.

  • The fry · kachori or bedai going into the kadhai, the bubbles, the puff. Slow-mo the lift-out.
  • The jalebi coil · the batter spiral into ghee, then the syrup dip. The most hypnotic 8 seconds we own.
  • The lassi pour · thick malai-topped lassi into the kulhad, the kesar and pista drop on top.
  • The sabji moment · the ladle of aloo sabji onto the plate, the steam, the first break of the bedai.
  • The rabdi reduce · the milk thickening in the kadhai, the malai folds, the chill-and-serve.
  • Festival hero · in season: the ghewar soak, the modak pleat, the makhan churn, the peda shape, the panjiri roast.
  • The counter wide · the live counter working, the queue, the brand bag, the mor-pankh.
  • A face · one happy customer, one honest line to camera ("bilkul Mathura jaisa"). Ask first, always.
  • The hands · close on the founder's or staff's hands making something. No faces needed. Pure craft.
  • Stills · 30-40 plated stills on the terracotta and brand backdrop, with the feather, for carousels and creatives.

Capture rails to have live before the festivals: the WhatsApp channel, a hamper or pre-order link, and the Google review QR. The festival crowd is the cheapest audience we will ever build, but only if we can catch it.
