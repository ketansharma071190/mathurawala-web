Morning Nashta Reel Script

Film this at the counter, 9-10 AM.

The morning daypart is the key growth lever. One well-shot 30-second Reel of the nashta being made will outperform 10 static posts for new reach. This script takes 60 minutes to shoot and can run for weeks.

Reel: "Mathura ka subah ka nashta" (30 sec)

Hook (0-3s): Close-up of kachori being lifted from hot oil, oil dripping, sizzle sound. No text yet.

Beat 1 (3-8s): Kachori placed on a leaf plate, sabji poured from kadhai beside it. Steam rising.

Beat 2 (8-15s): The bedai coming out of oil alongside. Jalebi coiling in the other kadhai. Cut to kulhad lassi being poured thick.

Beat 3 (15-22s): Full plate wide shot: kachori, bedai, jalebi, kulhad lassi together. Someone's hand picks up the kachori. First bite.

End card (22-30s): Text: "Mathura ka asli nashta" + "9 AM se, Mathurawala, Baner" + mor-pankh logo. Audio fades to ambient sound.

Shot list (in order):
  • Overhead of kachori in hot oil (phone at edge of kadhai, sizzle audio)
  • Kachori being lifted with tongs (medium close)
  • Kachori on leaf plate with sabji being poured in
  • Bedai in oil, and one being placed beside kachori
  • Jalebi being coiled (spiral going in, close-up)
  • Kulhad lassi pour from above (slow, thick, pista on top)
  • Full plate aerial wide shot
  • Someone picks up and first bite (reaction optional)

Caption:

Subah 9 baje, Mathurawala mein... Kachori. Bedai. Jalebi. Kulhad lassi. Roz. Taaza. Mathura ke haathon se. Kab aa rahe ho? Baner, Pune. Radhe Radhe 🦚 #kachori #mathura #morningnashta #punefood #mathurawala #reels
