Jalebi Reel Script

Film the jalebi being made. 20 seconds. Unstoppable.

The single shot that will get the most shares: jalebi coiling in hot oil. It is visually hypnotic and 100% made-live proof. If only one Reel gets shot this week, this is it.

Reel: "Jab jalebi live banti hai" (20 sec)

Hook (0-3s): Extreme close-up of jalebi batter being piped in a spiral into hot oil. The coil forms. Sizzle audio at full volume. No text.

Beat 1 (3-10s): Pull back slightly. Two or three jalebis coiling. Golden orange, oil bubbling. Medium close, slight overhead angle.

Beat 2 (10-15s): Ladle lifts a finished jalebi out. Dripping oil. Placed on a leaf or plate. Steam. Cut to sugar syrup being drizzled.

End card (15-20s): Jalebi on plate, hand picks one up, a small bite. Text overlay: "Kadhai se seedha. Roz. Mathurawala, Baner." Fade to mor-pankh logo on navy.

Caption:

Jab jalebi live banti hai... Koi frozen nahi. Koi reheated nahi. Har plate kadhai se seedha. Mathurawala, Baner, Pune. Khao toh asli khao. 🦚 #jalebi #reelsfood #baner #punefood #madeLive #mathurawala

Audio tip: Use natural sizzle audio from the shoot itself. Do not add music track. The sound of oil alone is the hook.
