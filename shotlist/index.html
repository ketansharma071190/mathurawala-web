Capture Shot List · one 60-90 min session

How to shoot: phone, vertical (9:16) for Reels and Stories, a few horizontal too. Keep each clip 8 to 20 seconds, 3-4 takes each. Wipe the lens, hold steady (or prop the phone), get close, shoot in the counter light. Start ON the action (the puff, the pour) so the first second hooks. Send all the raw clips and photos back to me in this chat and I turn them into 4-5 Reels plus a week of posts. Approx is fine, more is better. Radhe Radhe 🙏
