Logo

The wordmark on the flute, with the mor-pankh.

The master logo is the "mathurawala" wordmark resting on Krishna's bansuri (flute), with the complete peacock feather and its tassel at the end. The feather is the brand's signature mark and should keep its colour wherever possible.

Primary · dark wordmark, on light/cream backgrounds
Reversed · white wordmark + coloured feather, on navy/dark

Do

  • Use the dark logo on cream/light, the reversed one on navy/dark
  • Keep the mor-pankh in colour (teal, blue, gold)
  • Give it clear space; let it breathe
  • Use the feather alone as the app/favicon mark

Don't

  • Don't put the dark logo on a dark background (or vice versa)
  • Don't flatten the feather to one colour or white when colour is possible
  • Don't stretch, rotate, recolour the wordmark, or add effects
  • Don't crowd it with text or clip the feather

Typography

Fraunces for soul, Inter for clarity.

Mathura ka asli swad.
Fraunces italic, for the Braj accents and pull-quotes
Fraunces (serif) carries every headline and the heritage feeling, weights 800 to 900. Inter carries all body and UI text (this paragraph), weights 400 to 600. Kickers are Inter, uppercase, letter-spaced, in gold or teal. The logo wordmark is a bold high-contrast serif in this same family, so Fraunces Black at large sizes echoes it. For Hindi and Braj lines we set Tiro Devanagari Hindi, so Devanagari reads intentional, never system-default.

Voice and tone

Authentic, proud, made live.

We sound like a confident heritage brand that happens to be deeply warm, not like a humble local kitchen. Rooted, premium, a little reverent (Krishna, Mathura), and human. We use Braj Bhasha touches (Radhe Radhe) for authenticity, never for cuteness.

The "ghar" rule (important)

Home is a seasoning, not the main dish.

Leaning on "ghar / homely / ghar jaisa / just like home" makes us read as a small comfort eatery and caps the brand. Lead instead with original, authentic, from the source, made live, pure, Mathura, the way it has always been made. The homesick-diaspora emotion is real and powerful, so use it sparingly and through customer voice (a real review), and frame the connection as the real thing from the source (pride), not as homely comfort. One well-placed note beats ten.

Words we lead with

  • asli, shuddh, original, pure
  • Mathura, Braj, Krishna's city
  • made live, fresh, the source, 5,000 years
  • the way Mathura makes it; the standard
  • always say what we ARE (assert the strength)
  • Radhe Radhe (sparing, authentic)

Words we limit or avoid

  • defining us by what we aren't ("not a...", "this is not that", "unlike")
  • homely, ghar jaisa, just like home (overused)
  • comfort food, cosy, humble
  • generic "authentic taste", "delicious", "yummy"
  • em dashes, ever; AI-sounding phrasing

Photography

Real, warm, made-live.

Real food on terracotta and in kulhads, warm light, the mor-pankh in frame, the craft visible (the flame, the ghee, the pour). Avoid anything that looks AI-generated, over-perfect, or stocky. If a shot looks fake, it is off-brand. When we do not have a real photo of an item yet, use a clean typographic treatment in the palette rather than a fake image.
