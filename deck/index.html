The truth nobody is saying

The world eats more lifeless food every year. Bharat invented the alternative.

Bharat made food fresh, pure and live for 5,000 years, before most of today's nations existed. Italian food has a dozen global brands. Japanese, a dozen. Bharat's living vegetarian food has no global standard-bearer. The most authentic cuisine on earth has no flag-bearer brand.

The unclaimed flag

Nobody owns Mathura. We do.

Mathura is Krishna's birthplace and one of the world's oldest living cities. Braj cuisine is a real, named, beloved heritage cuisine, and the single most emotionally loaded, commercially unclaimed food banner in India. The brand is literally named for the place.

What we are

Not a restaurant chain. A heritage food brand that scales the proven way.

The outlet engine · the scaler

Outlets and kiosks, dine-in and takeaway. The proof, the experience, the daily cash, and where the brand and content are born. Governed density across a city, then more cities.

The central kitchen · freshness, at scale

Made fresh every day from one kitchen, served across outlets within a freshness radius, so the taste never drifts as we grow. That same kitchen lets us move staff to where demand is and take the bulk and corporate orders we turn away today. Freshness is the promise; the leverage is how it scales.

Why now, and in what order

The window is open, and the prize is sequenced.

Three forces have lined up at once. We capture them in an obvious order: prove the outlet model and the central-kitchen freshness first, expand city by city, and let packaged D2C follow as the natural next step.

Now

The window is open

Backlash against frozen, preservative food. Bhartiya pride is aspirational again. And a fresh heritage brand can finally be built fast. We already have the proven outlet, the brand and the demand.

Year 1-2

Central kitchen + outlet density

A central kitchen, a flagship and kiosks across Pune, made fresh, served fresh. Unlock the bulk and corporate orders we turn away today. Drive our own footfall and repeat, so we own the customer, not the aggregator.

Year 3+

More cities, then D2C, obviously

Replicate the proven city model elsewhere. As that volume builds, packaging and manufacturing make peda, mithai D2C and gifting the obvious next step, a natural extension of what the kitchens already make, not a leap.
