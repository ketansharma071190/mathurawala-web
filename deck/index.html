The size of the prize

~₹1 lakh Cr

India's sweets and savouries category, and still overwhelmingly unorganised. Trust and a named brand are the opening, exactly what a heritage brand provides.

Haldiram's valuation in 2025, the largest in India's packaged-food history. Bikaji is a ~₹17,000 Cr listed company. The ceiling for an Indian heritage food brand is enormous, and proven.

Those giants are packaged-led and rooted in the North and West. Nobody has built the fresh, made-live heritage brand around Mathura and Braj. And winning takes only a small share: at ~₹1.1 Cr per outlet, a few dozen outlets with mother outlets, bulk and gifting reach ₹50-100 Cr, a rounding error against the category and these comps. The prize is enormous and proven; the work is execution, and that is exactly what we do.
