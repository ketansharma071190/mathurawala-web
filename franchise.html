Formats

Two expansion lanes, one brand standard.

We will evaluate the model after we understand the catchment, rent, footprint, visibility and partner role.

Full outlet

Neighbourhood Mathurawala

Snacks, meals, sweets, namkeen, gifting, dine-in and delivery under one operating system.

  • Best for dense residential, high-street and family catchments.
  • Needs kitchen depth, visible frontage and repeat local demand.
  • Can be partner-owned and company-operated where it makes sense.
Kiosk or counter

Campus and compact format

A focused menu built around fast-moving snacks, chaat, meal plates, sweets and beverages.

  • Best for IT parks, campuses, offices and tight high-footfall zones.
  • Lower footprint, tighter menu and faster service design.
  • Useful for multi-site rollouts once supply and training are steady.
Cluster partner

City build-out

For serious partners who can help build a city cluster without weakening food or brand control.

  • Pune first, then Mumbai, Bengaluru and other high-fit markets.
  • Revenue-share, franchise, debt or equity can be discussed case by case.
  • Brand standards, vendor quality and operations stay Mathurawala-led.
