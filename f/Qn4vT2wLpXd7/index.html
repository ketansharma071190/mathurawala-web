Gas is fixed, and that is the whole story

The single biggest reason the old P&L looked healthy. The costing master allows ₹3 a plate for deep-fry, ₹2 tawa, ₹2.5 simmer, ₹6 milk-reduce. The LPG WhatsApp group records what is actually bought and paid: nine dated invoices from 11 July to 4 August totalling 24 cylinders, every one paid the same or the next day. Net of the opening delivery that is 21 cylinders consumed across those 24 days, 0.93 a day. That is 17.7 kg of gas a day for a 15-hour halwai kitchen. An earlier read of this same group put the rate at 1.27 a day; that counted five deliveries across four intervals and overstated the line by about ₹28,424 a month.

Why a delivery rupee is worth a third of a counter rupee

We receive about 52% of the online menu price after commission, merchant-funded discount and ads. To bank the same rupee as the counter the markup would have to be 92%. Actual markups run 21–49%, median about 39%. A 39% markup does not survive a 48% deduction, because the markup is on the base and the deduction is on the marked-up price.
