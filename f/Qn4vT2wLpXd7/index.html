The gas hole

The single biggest reason the old P&L looked healthy. The costing master allows ₹3 a plate for deep-fry, ₹2 tawa, ₹2.5 simmer, ₹6 milk-reduce. The LPG WhatsApp group records what is actually bought and paid: 14 cylinders between 21 July and 1 August, 1.27 a day. Cross-checked bottom-up against burner hours (24.1 kg/day measured against ~26 kg/day modelled for a 15-hour halwai kitchen), so the number is real, not a mis-read.

Why a delivery rupee is worth a third of a counter rupee

We receive about 52% of the online menu price after commission, merchant-funded discount and ads. To bank the same rupee as the counter the markup would have to be 92%. Actual markups run 21–49%, median about 39%. A 39% markup does not survive a 48% deduction, because the markup is on the base and the deduction is on the marked-up price.
