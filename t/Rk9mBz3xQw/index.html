WHAT WAS STOPPED, AND WHAT REPLACED IT

This is the part that matters commercially.

Before 31 July, we were running: a daily Instagram feed post at 10:00 with an approved queue,

a daily Google Business Profile update, every Google review answered inside 24 hours, and a daily

Kanha board line photographed and pushed to stories.

We stood all of it down on 31 July so that TDP could take the channels over cleanly and we

would not be publishing on top of them.

What has run since: nothing. Instagram has not moved off 167 posts. GBP is dark. Reviews are

going unanswered. The one board-story obligation they were carrying just recorded its first miss.

The uncomfortable control result: Sunday 2 August was the **biggest day in the entire tracked

history, ₹57,396 across 226 bills**, and it happened with both channels dark. So their absence is

not yet visibly costing revenue. That cuts both ways, and it is worth saying out loud rather than

pretending the sky fell. But we are paying a retainer for a function that is currently not

operating at all, and the festive window is the point at which absence starts to cost real money.

The clock that actually matters: Rakhi is 24 days out, Janmashtami 31 days. The shoot that was

supposed to gate the festive arc happened 8 days ago and we have not seen a single frame.

MY READ ON WHY

Not incompetence, and worth saying so. The pattern is specific and consistent:

• They engage on calls and go silent in writing. Kickoff call, no recap. Second meet, no

summary. Every single written-deliverable obligation is at "never met", while every verbal

touchpoint went fine.

• They commit to dates and then do not grade themselves against them. Mehul's own words on

21 July were "by tomorrow, first thing in the morning". That date passed 13 days ago.

• Their inbound is asks, not output. Today's only message was a request for photographs from

us. Over 15 days the traffic has been predominantly us handing over and them requesting.

None of that requires bad faith. It looks like an agency that has taken the account, done the

strategy layer, and not yet staffed the execution layer. **Which is exactly the thing to name

plainly, early, and in writing, before the festive season makes it expensive.**

Do not terminate. Do not threaten. Three reasons: the slab deal is cheap at the base tier, the

they have not.

Do this instead, in order:
