What a second outlet changes

Rent and salaries repeat. The brand, recipes, costing, supplier rates and this board do not — they are already built and carry across at no extra cost. That is the difference between opening a second shop and starting a second business.

Known gaps, stated plainly

Missed bills. Card and UPI are both keyed as “Card”, and PetPooja has recorded UPI as zero for three years. Paytm banked ₹3,97,820 in July; the gap between that and what PetPooja billed is sales taken with no bill punched. One payment-mix export sizes it exactly.
